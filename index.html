Why You Need a Supercharger Rebuild

Your SeaDoo supercharger is one of the most stressed components on your watercraft. Ignoring it is a costly mistake.

⚠️

Ceramic Washers

Older SeaDoos came with ceramic washers that disintegrate and destroy your engine. If you still have them — rebuild immediately.

⏱️

100 Hour Rule

SeaDoo recommends supercharger service every 100 hours. Don't wait for failure — a rebuild is far cheaper than a new engine.

💨

44,000 RPM

Your supercharger spins at over 44,000 RPM. At those speeds, worn bearings and seals fail fast and catastrophically.

💰

Protect Your Investment

A supercharger rebuild costs a fraction of engine replacement. Don't let a $600 service turn into a $6,000 repair.
